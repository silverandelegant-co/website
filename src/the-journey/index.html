Establishing the design practice in Hertfordshire, implementing the custom generative algorithms and digital render pipelines that define our visual aesthetics.

Serving as official jewellery partner for Paul Costelloe's Autumn/Winter 2026 collection opening show at The Waldorf Hilton, London. Fusing sculptural bronze drops with runway couture.
