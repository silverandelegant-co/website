The Maker

Silver is patient.
So is the work.

Every piece begins not with metal but with an idea — sometimes a line in a sketchbook, sometimes a shape noticed in the world. Silver & Elegant was built on the belief that jewellery should carry its own history.

Made in a small studio with hand tools, each piece passes through dozens of decisions before it is finished. The form, the finish, the weight — nothing is incidental. The work takes the time it takes.

Silver & Elegant jewellery is available on Wolf & Badger, selected by their team as part of their collection of independent designers.

The Process
01

Sketch

Each piece begins as a drawing — a study of form, proportion, and intention before a single tool is picked up.

02

Form

Sterling silver sheet or wire is cut, shaped, and worked by hand. No two sessions produce the same result — the material makes its own decisions.

03

Finish

The surface is treated to reveal its character — polished, oxidised, or left with the texture of the hammer. Nothing is erased.
